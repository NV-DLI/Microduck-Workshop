The course structure was informed by [NemoClawDLI](https://github.com/NVDLI/NemoClawDLI), NVIDIA DLI c-fx-30-v1, and the supplied sim2real tutorial. Their private archives, credentials, and course source were not copied into this repository. Lessons and frontend implementation here are newly authored.

The 3D model reproduces the provided CAD revision, including its electronics envelopes. It does not assert that older Raspberry Pi / Robot HAT geometry depicts the newer electronics used by the simulation sensor configuration.

Community demonstrations: `community-jump-hd.mp4` is derived from [Thomas Burgess / microduck-max-height-jump](https://github.com/ThomasBurgess2000/microduck-max-height-jump), retaining its four-times slow motion. `community-balance-hd.mp4` is derived from [Hannes von Essen / microduck-playground](https://github.com/Vottivott/microduck-playground), basketball preview, seed 0. Both repositories use Apache-2.0; robot-geometry terms remain separate as above. Videos are re-encoded and poster frames extracted. `community-flamingo-hd.mp4` is derived from the preview of [Rémi Fabre / microduck-flamingo-cycle](https://huggingface.co/RemiFabre/microduck-flamingo-cycle) (`media/preview.mp4` at revision `a69e4f6103a70e355983eb5d4e919da76778a40f`, SHA-256 `0cc129cd3ae49fe463bd6808c6f3cb6fe8eb86a7307acc8cb5c88c8d90461ee2`), Apache-2.0 per its model card; re-encoded without audio at the original 640×480 resolution, with a poster frame at 6 s. They represent the original community environments, not a new Newton or hardware validation.

Shared indoor scene: `lab/assets/` includes the official full-contact robot conversion and original parametric steel-goal/mat assets. Dimensions, assumptions and source attribution are in [lab/assets/README.md](lab/assets/README.md). The indoor detector is a fine-tuned derivative of the preserved course detector under the same Ultralytics AGPL-3.0 terms.

Official browser assets: `models/official-browser/` preserves Pollen Robotics simulator policies from [revision ad9d41a763f30dc770817c33d92dcfec0205db1e](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollen-robotics/microduck-simulator/tree/ad9d41a763f30dc770817c33d92dcfec0205db1e), with source URLs and SHA-256 hashes in its manifest. The official Space license is Apache-2.0, saved in `licenses/official-simulator-Apache-2.0.txt`. `artwork/build_official_ball.py` ports its procedural `ball-visual.js` panel layout and colours to a metric USD sphere. The ball retains the website diameter (10 cm) and mass (30 g). Roller CAD remains subject to the separate Pollen Robotics geometry terms above.

Interactive teaching diagrams: the supplied `sim-to-real-so-101-workshop-newton` workshop (Copyright © 2026 NVIDIA CORPORATION & AFFILIATES, Apache-2.0) informed the selectable-node, connector-highlighting and explanation-panel interaction. Its license is retained at `licenses/SO-101-reference-Apache-2.0.txt`. The Microduck SVG renderer, node positions, bilingual explanations, responsive sizing and event handling are rewritten for this course. No private telemetry, course archive, or reference training data is redistributed.

Terrain lab appearance: `lab/course_labs/terrain_studio.py` uses the base-colour texture of NVIDIA's Omniverse base material `Materials/Base/Wood/Plywood.mdl` from the Isaac Sim asset library (the same library Isaac Lab's examples use). The material folder is downloaded at run time into the user's cache and is not redistributed in this repository; it remains under the Isaac Sim asset terms linked above. The texture is applied to non-colliding display meshes and as a visual binding only; terrain geometry and physics materials are unchanged.
