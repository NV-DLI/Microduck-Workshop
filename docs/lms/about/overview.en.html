Use NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab to teach the open-source Microduck biped new skills in simulation. Train walking and slope traversal with PPO, domain randomization and reward design, then run a hand-designed onboard policy that scores a walking shot. Finally, see how NVIDIA Isaac GR00T learns the walking shot from demonstrations.

Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, GR00T

  • Basic Python experience and familiarity with the Ubuntu command line.
  • NVIDIA DGX Spark with Ubuntu for the labs.

After completing this course, you will be able to:

  • Run Microduck simulations in Isaac Sim and inspect a policy's observations, actions and control cycle.
  • Explain the basics of PPO and run training and validation of a walking policy with Isaac Lab and Newton.
  • Design rewards and domain randomization, and judge training results from TensorBoard curves and separate task evaluations.
  • Explain how an onboard policy connects YOLO detection, ToF ranging, a straight approach and motion skills, and analyze shot outcomes.
  • Explain how GR00T divides a walking shot between 8-D high-level requests and trained low-level skills, and how demonstrations, action-head training and closed-loop validation work.
  • Explain the generalization challenges a model faces in new environments, and how environment randomization can help it adapt to those changes.

The table below lists the main topics of the course's six parts, which you can work through at your own pace.

1. Overview
  • Learn the course route and objectives
  • Build and enter the course container
  • Confirm that Isaac Sim and the GPU work
2. Meet Microduck
  • Walk and kick with Microduck from the keyboard in Isaac Sim
  • Explore the robot's structure, joints, actuators and sensors
  • Understand the policy's control cycle
3. Train skills with reinforcement learning
  • Understand the basics of reinforcement learning and PPO
  • Map observations, actions, rewards, Actor and Critic onto Microduck walking
  • Train and test a walking policy with Isaac Lab and Newton, and see what domain randomization contributes
  • Extension: design rewards that keep Microduck steady on slopes
  • (Extension) Reward design in community skills: one-leg standing and basketball balance
4. Shoot with an onboard policy
  • Use RGB, ToF and a compact YOLO detector to identify the ball and goal and estimate distances
  • Connect motion skills with a straight approach and feedback adjustments, repeat shots and analyze successes and failures
5. (Extension) Score autonomously with GR00T
  • Understand vision-language-action (VLA) models and GR00T
  • See how GR00T scores a walking shot with 8-D high-level requests and trained skills
  • Understand the demonstrations, action-head training and closed-loop validation behind it
  • Discuss how environment randomization can help a model adapt to new environments
6. Reference and help
  • Look up the course models and data
  • Troubleshoot common problems
  • Find references for further learning
