Complete a taskConnect perception, localization, planning, walking and kicking

A motion actor solves how to move. A complete system also decides what to do, where the target is, whether information is reliable and when to switch skills. Keep demonstrations, skill benchmarks and task-level evaluation distinct.

Challenge: slopes, uneven ground and obstacles

Suppose the pitch has slopes, uneven patches and obstacles. Can the robot still score? This is a design challenge only; it is not implemented in the course.

ChangeQuestions to investigate
SlopesCan the actor balance? Does the ball roll before contact?
Uneven groundIs projection onto z = 0 valid? Is local surface height or depth needed?
ObstaclesHow can a map provide clearance and support replanning?
Inclined kick surfaceAre the flat-ground starting pose, ball relation and recovery conditions still valid?
More complex taskHow can perception, navigation and motion failures be separated?

Draft a one-page proposal: scene variables, sensors, reusable policies, new training needs, data splits, success criteria and compute budget. Use Isaac Sim for scenes and Isaac Lab for terrain and skill evaluation.

One final question

Check your understanding

Why do slope walking and flat-pitch shooting not establish slope-pitch autonomous shooting?

Show explanation

The combined task changes ball motion, localization assumptions and kicking conditions. It needs its own end-to-end evaluation.
