SELF-PACED WORKSHOP

Teach Microduck to score a goal

Start with a small biped. Use simulation, reinforcement learning, and visual perception to turn seeing a football into a complete autonomous action.

7 chapters · 中文 / English · Simulation labs

What you will build

The final scene contains one Microduck, one football, and one goal. The robot must find the ball, locate the goal, approach a useful kicking position, make contact, and recover its stance. You will learn what each part of this system does and how the parts work together.

  1. 1Understand the mechanism
  2. 2Control and train
  3. 3See and navigate
  4. 4Score autonomously

By the end, you can

  • Explain how joints, actuators, observations, actions, and rewards relate.
  • Launch a Newton / Isaac Lab simulation and inspect a fixed policy in action.
  • Run a bounded training exercise, locate checkpoints, and interpret training curves.
  • Connect camera detections, ToF ranges, and body attitude to autonomous task states.
  • Record both successes and failures, distinguishing model metrics from task metrics.
