SELF-PACED WORKSHOP

Teach Microduck to score a goal

Explore motion and training, then move from engineered football to GR00T, ASPIRE and Cosmos: learning actions, organizing tasks and adapting to new environments.

9 chapters · 中文 / English · Simulation and frontier methods

What you will build

Use one Microduck, one ball and one goal to connect motion, simulation and learning with engineered pipelines, learned policies and task agents. Chapters 1–5 retain existing labs, Chapter 6 reviews the baseline, and Chapters 7–9 explain frontier methods and readiness. New-method football training has not yet been completed here.

  1. 1Motion and learning foundations
  2. 2Review the engineered baseline
  3. 3Learn actions with GR00T
  4. 4Organize tasks with ASPIRE
  5. 5Expand environments with Cosmos and Isaac

By the end, you can

  • Explain mechanisms, actuators, observations, actions and rewards.
  • Explore fixed policies and learning with Isaac Lab, Newton and Isaac Sim.
  • Distinguish engineered pipelines, VLA actions and task agents.
  • Adapt the SO-101 data workflow to Microduck demonstration/action requirements.
  • Design variation and held-out evaluation; distinguish readiness from task success.
